## The Question Every Student Asks

You can learn AI alone with courses and tutorials, but it's slower and lonelier. So is a community worth joining, and is it worth leading one?

My honest answer: you can learn alone, but you'll go faster with people. Leading a chapter isn't for everyone, but it gives you things no course can.

## What a Chapter Gives You

- A meetup every two weeks, offline or online
- Short sessions, so no one needs to give up a whole evening
- Peers from other colleges who share your interest in AI
- A clear path into open source
- Tracked hours, certificates, and rewards

We're starting in Chennai, Coimbatore, and Trichy (coming soon). The program is new, so details may change after the first three-month evaluation. Check the latest announcement before applying.

## What a Meetup Looks Like

Each session runs 50 minutes at most:

1. **Random fun challenge** to break the ice
2. **AI talk** on one topic or tool
3. **Fun games** to keep the energy up
4. **Open-source discussion** on how to start contributing

Attendance is recorded every time. The idea is simple: make friends first, learn along the way.

## Member or Ambassador?

**Stay a regular member** if you want less commitment and just want to learn. You can still earn the participation certificate: 50 hours of activity, including 5 hours of open source.

**Become an ambassador** if you enjoy meeting people and want leadership experience. Each chapter needs at least one ambassador every month. Your job is to:

- Invite students from your college
- Welcome newcomers so nobody sits alone
- Help the host run the session
- Handle small things like snacks
- Encourage people to join talks, games, and open-source work

Ambassadors get a stipend of INR 500 to INR 2,000, plus a bonus after completing 50 hours of work.

## Rewards at a Glance

- **Participation certificate:** 50 hours, with 5 in open source
- **Milestone recognition:** one member picked for every 100 hours completed
- **Quarterly top performer:** one member across all chapters gets a certificate, a bonus, and INR 5,000

## A Middle Path: Join First, Lead Later

Attend a few meetups as a member, help out informally, and apply once you know you enjoy it. You don't need to be an AI expert. Curiosity, reliability, and the willingness to bring people together matter more.

## How to Apply

Send us your name, college, preferred chapter, why you want to be an ambassador, and how you'd get students at your college involved. Keep it honest and specific.

## My Take

Go to two or three meetups before deciding. One real conversation with another student teaches more than a week of solo tutorials. If you find yourself naturally making others feel included, that's your signal to apply.

## Quick Checklist

- Meetups: 50 minutes, every two weeks
- At least one ambassador needed per chapter each month
- Stipend: INR 500 to INR 2,000
- Certificate: 50 hours, including 5 hours of open source
- Quarterly prize: INR 5,000
- No expertise needed to apply

## Closing Thought

Joining and leading are different commitments, and neither is better. Membership gives you company and structure. Ambassadorship gives you leadership and a community with your fingerprints on it.

Whichever you choose, remember what makes a chapter work: be the one who starts the conversation.
